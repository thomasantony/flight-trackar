Privacy Policy — FlightTrackAR

Effective Date: November 22, 2026  ·  Package: com.thomasantony.flighttrackar  ·  Developer: Thomas Antony

1. Overview

FlightTrackAR is an augmented-reality flight-tracking app for Android that overlays live, openly available ADS-B flight positions onto your device's camera view. FlightTrackAR contains no advertisements, no analytics SDKs, no user accounts, and no developer-operated backend servers.

2. Data Processed by the App

  • Camera (CAMERA): Used strictly to show the live camera preview and perform real-time, on-device visual aircraft detection. Camera frames are processed transiently in RAM only and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted off your device.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used to calculate the 3D bearing, elevation, and distance of overhead flights, compute local magnetic declination, and request nearby flights. When live tracking is active (outside Demo Simulation mode), your device sends HTTPS requests containing your rounded coordinates (4 decimal places) and search radius directly to the open ADS-B feed you select: opendata.adsb.fi, api.adsb.lol, or opensky-network.org.
  • Motion & Orientation Sensors: Accelerometer, gyroscope, and magnetometer readings are processed strictly on-device to orient the AR overlay and never leave your device.

3. Data Sharing & Retention

The developer does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. App preferences remain locally on your device and are removed when the app is uninstalled or cleared.

4. Children's Privacy

FlightTrackAR does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
